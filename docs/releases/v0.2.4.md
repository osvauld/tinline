# Tinline v0.2.4

Voice messages in groups, every account on your phone reachable, and contact requests that reach a sleeping phone.

## New

- **Voice messages in groups.** Record one in any group you can post in, on Android, desktop and iOS; everyone can play it.
- **Every account can be reached.** With several accounts on one phone (Play edition), a message or call to an account
  you don't have open still wakes the phone; the notification says which account it's for, and the account switcher
  shows an unread dot. Answering a call on another account switches to it. On desktop all accounts stay connected.
  Locking the app keeps every account shut.
- **Contact requests reach a sleeping phone.** Your contact code now carries a wake-up, so a request arrives even
  while the other phone sleeps; once you're contacts, you can wake each other straight away.

## Changed

- Contact codes and their QR codes are longer. **Codes made with v0.2.3 or earlier no longer work: make a new one.**
- Wake-ups for an unanswered request slow down over time (30 s, 2 min, 10 min, 30 min, then hourly) instead of every
  30 seconds.
- Android: every new message in a conversation alerts again; a reply you send from the notification stays silent.
- Android: the chat follows your newest message after you send.
- Desktop: catching up after being away no longer sets off a burst of old alerts; short notices clear themselves;
  photos fit their bubble.

## Fixed

- Android: a message's heads-up no longer vanishes early (the background service notification was grouped with it).
- Android: the chat no longer crashes after a call that was logged twice.

## Downloads

- **Android, two editions** (arm64-v8a and x86_64), both signed with the same key. Check them against `SHA256SUMS`.
  - `tinline-play-v0.2.4.apk`: wake-ups through Google. Needs Google Play services; pick this one on most phones.
  - `tinline-liberum-v0.2.4.apk`: no Google code at all; stays connected all the time.
  - **Coming from an earlier version:** install the same edition you have; it updates your app and keeps your data.
- **Linux:** `.deb`, AppImage and tarball, built on Ubuntu 24.04.

## Known limits

- A message reaches a sleeping phone only while the sender's app is still running.
- Desktop and Liberum have no wake-ups. Group calls and posts don't wake a sleeping member yet.
- No iOS release.
- Some text that comes from the shared core (call log lines, a few status cues) is still in English.
