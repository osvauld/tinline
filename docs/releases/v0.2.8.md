# Tinline v0.2.8

Group photos open full-size, and the newest message stays in view while you type.

## Changed

- Groups: tap a photo to open it full-size. Pinch, double-tap or drag to zoom (Android); wheel and drag on the
  desktop, with arrows to step through the group's photos. Save it to your phone or to Downloads, or share it.
- Photos in 1:1 chats zoom the same way.
- Group notifications show each sender's picture (their initial when they have none).

## Fixed

- In a group, opening the keyboard or typing several lines hid the newest messages behind the text box. The list now
  keeps the newest message in view while you are at the bottom, and stays put while you read older messages. A
  1:1 chat follows the same rule.
- A voice call started on the loudspeaker when the call before it had ended there. It now starts at your ear;
  video and group calls still start on the loudspeaker, and a headset still wins.

## Downloads

- **Android, two editions** (arm64-v8a), both signed with the same key. Check them against `SHA256SUMS`.
  - `tinline-play-v0.2.8.apk`: wake-ups through Google. Needs Google Play services; pick this one on most phones.
  - `tinline-liberum-v0.2.8.apk`: no Google code at all; stays connected all the time.
  - **Coming from an earlier version:** install the same edition you have; it updates your app and keeps your data.
- **Linux:** `.deb`, AppImage and tarball, built on Ubuntu 24.04.

## Known limits

- Desktop: after opening a group's info, the next group you open can show its info instead of the chat. Click the
  group's name to go back to the chat. Fixed in the next version.
- Desktop: the zoom level carries over when stepping to the next photo.
- A message reaches a sleeping phone only while the sender's app is still running.
- Desktop and Liberum have no wake-ups (they can still wake your phone). Group calls and posts don't wake a
  sleeping member yet.
- No iOS release.
- Some text that comes from the shared core (call log lines, a few status cues) is still in English.
