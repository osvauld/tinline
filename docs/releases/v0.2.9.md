# Tinline v0.2.9

Send files in groups, get your chat history back from your contacts, and lists scroll smoothly.

## Changed

- Groups: send any file up to 2 GB, with a caption. Each file has its own card: Download, then Open, Save to your
  phone or Downloads, or Delete my copy. A line says who else has it ("B is also hosting this file"). Group hosts keep
  every file, so it stays available while the sender is offline.
- 1:1 chats: a contact now carries the whole conversation, your own messages included. A laptop that was shut for
  days catches up through the contact even when your phone is off, and restoring your account from the 24 words
  brings your 1:1 history back from each contact that is online. Older days come in the background, newest first.
  Each message is still checked against its author's signature, so a contact
  can't change or add your words.
- **Everyone needs this version to keep chatting.** 1:1 chats use a new connection version; an older app can't
  exchange messages with 0.2.9 until it is updated. Nothing stored is lost.

## Fixed

- Desktop: after opening a group's info, opening a group again showed its info instead of the chat. A group now
  always opens on its conversation.
- Android: tapping a group's notification brought back Group info if you had left it open. It now opens that group's
  conversation.
- Android: the chat list stuttered while scrolling on slower phones. The fingerprint pictures are now drawn once
  instead of on every frame.

## Downloads

- **Android, two editions** (arm64-v8a), both signed with the same key. Check them against `SHA256SUMS`.
  - `tinline-play-v0.2.9.apk`: wake-ups through Google. Needs Google Play services; pick this one on most phones.
  - `tinline-liberum-v0.2.9.apk`: no Google code at all; stays connected all the time.
  - **Coming from an earlier version:** install the same edition you have; it updates your app and keeps your data.
- **Linux:** `.deb`, AppImage and tarball, built on Ubuntu 24.04.

## Known limits

- Files in groups are not in the iOS app yet. Older apps can't open a group day that holds a file post.
- Android: opening a very large file in another app first copies it, which takes a while.
- Catching up on older days (chats and groups) also runs on mobile data and low battery; the apps don't pause it yet.
- Desktop: the zoom level carries over when stepping to the next photo.
- A message reaches a sleeping phone only while the sender's app is still running.
- Desktop and Liberum have no wake-ups (they can still wake your phone). Group calls and posts don't wake a
  sleeping member yet.
- No iOS release.
- Some text that comes from the shared core (call log lines, a few status cues) is still in English.
