# Tinline v0.2.7

Your phone and computer now keep each other up to date, even while the phone sleeps.

## Fixed

- Your computer wakes your sleeping phone when it has something new for it (a message it relayed, read state, a
  contact's new profile picture). The wake-up is content-free, like a contact's; at most one every 30 seconds.
  Both devices need this version.
- Delivered and read ticks match on all your devices. Before, a message sent from the phone but delivered through
  the computer stayed on "waiting" on the phone. Ticks come from the contact's signed receipts, passed on unchanged.
- A contact's new profile picture reaches every one of your devices, also when the contact is offline.
- A picture that fails to download is tried again as soon as your devices reconnect, and the reason is logged.

## Downloads

- **Android, two editions** (arm64-v8a), both signed with the same key. Check them against `SHA256SUMS`.
  - `tinline-play-v0.2.7.apk`: wake-ups through Google. Needs Google Play services; pick this one on most phones.
  - `tinline-liberum-v0.2.7.apk`: no Google code at all; stays connected all the time.
  - **Coming from an earlier version:** install the same edition you have; it updates your app and keeps your data.
- **Linux:** `.deb`, AppImage and tarball, built on Ubuntu 24.04.

## Known limits

- A message reaches a sleeping phone only while the sender's app is still running.
- Desktop and Liberum have no wake-ups (they can still wake your phone). Group calls and posts don't wake a
  sleeping member yet.
- No iOS release.
- Some text that comes from the shared core (call log lines, a few status cues) is still in English.
