# Tinline v0.2.0

The first release since v0.1.31. Tinline is a personal, peer-to-peer line to your people: chat and calls go
directly between devices over iroh, with no account server. This is an early build to try with friends,
ideally on a spare Android phone. It is not a finished product.

**v0.2.0 does not talk to v0.1.x.** The wire formats and stored data changed. Everyone you test with needs
v0.2.0, and an existing v0.1.x install should be removed first.

## New

- **Group chat.** Invites and short links, chat requests, image posts, and group history that syncs while
  you were away.
- **Public groups.** Open links, admins-only posting ("Not posted: only admins post here"), and who is
  around right now.
- **Group calls.** Up to 12 people on a desktop host, 4 on a phone and 2 on a metered connection.
- **Video calls.** 1:1 video with AV1 (libaom real-time encode, rav1d decode), and hardware VP8 on Android
  phones that have it. Quality adapts automatically, from 360p up to HD. The camera turns on only when you
  press for it; plain Answer keeps it off.
- **Search.** Find messages across your chats, and find within one chat.
- **Endpoint rotation.** Your network address changes on a schedule you set, while your account stays the same.
- **Blocking.** Visible blocking that you control, kept separate from group membership.
- **Devices.** Link a device with a QR code, see your device list, rename and unlink devices.
- **Receipts.** Delivered and read ticks for chats, and read and call receipts in groups.
- **Accounts.** Several accounts online at once, a login switcher, a recovery key and calmer recovery from
  a QR code.
- **Android calls.** Calls go through the system phone UI (Telecom), with Bluetooth and wired headset routing.
- **Idle links.** Connections to people close after 60 s idle and reopen when needed, with consent-based
  presence.

## Changed

- Message history is stored in day shards, so long histories load and sync faster.
- A new design across Android and desktop: icons, motion, the call strip, and copy in one caring voice.
- Errors that used to be swallowed are now logged or shown, and release builds log less.

## Downloads

- **Android:** `tinline-v0.2.0.apk` (arm64-v8a and x86_64), signed. Check it against `SHA256SUMS`.
- **Linux:** `.deb`, AppImage and tarball, built on Ubuntu 24.04. Older distributions are not verified.

## Known limits

- Not yet tested on real phones: public groups, idle links and recovery from a QR code.
- Some automated tests fail at random under load (listed in `docs/TODO.md`). Group calls with 12 people
  talking at once fail a capacity test.
- There is no iOS release. The iOS app builds for the simulator only.
- No anonymity promise: people you talk to can see your network address. Read the trust model before
  inviting people you don't know.
