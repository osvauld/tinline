Try the edges, not just the happy path

  • Use the keyboard, cancel a dialog, then try again.
  • Send while offline. Come back to an unfinished draft.
  • Listen to a ring, then answer or end it. Silence should be immediate.
  • Check what a block means in a shared group.

A design proposal, not a product claim

These local prototypes use simulated contacts and state. They do not place calls, send messages or change your account. Native sound, permissions and transport behavior still require app implementation.

The original Claude Design boards remain available. These studies add interaction detail without replacing the protocol decisions.
