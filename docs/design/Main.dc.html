Direct Relayed · encrypted Good Poor
Direct = solid line between two points (the tin-can string). Relayed = the line passes through a locked box: the relay forwards sealed packets and cannot listen. Quality = 4 bars from RTT + loss: ≥3 Good, 2 OK, 1 Poor. Always pair colour with the word.
