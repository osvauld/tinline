Open gaps on Android

  • Real phone ↔ laptop test. Task 31 is only emulator and demo checked.
  • Photo viewer zoom and swipe. One fit-to-screen photo; no pinch, no next or previous.
  • Audio and video playback in Tinline. They open in another app; only voice messages play in the bubble.
  • Download setting. The 25 MB “wait for tap” limit exists in core, with no Settings control.
  • Cancel or pause a transfer. Only progress, “paused”, and retry on failure exist.
  • Password-protected PDF. Shows an error; no way to enter a password.
