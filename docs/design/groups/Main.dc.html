A host is a desktop that keeps the group running when phones sleep. Your phone can’t host. Link your desktop, or ask a member to host from theirs after the group is made.

Only contacts can be added directly. You become the owner. A group needs at least one host before messages flow while you are offline; from a phone you pick a linked desktop or ask a member. Adding someone sends them an invite in your 1:1 chat.
