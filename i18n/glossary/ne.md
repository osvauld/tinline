# Nepali (ne), नेपाली

Read `india.md` first. Confidence: **medium-low**. Machine drafts often drift into
Hindi words and Hindi verb endings (है, करें) written in Devanagari.

## Script and layout
- Devanagari, left to right. Sentence end `।`.
- Digits: the OS may show Devanagari digits (०-९) for Nepali. Never type digits.
- Audience: Nepal, Sikkim, Darjeeling, Bhutan. Standard Nepali works for all.

## Register
**तपाईं** (middle honorific). Normal polite address in Nepali apps; हजुर is very
deferential, तिमी familiar, तँ intimate. Verbs at the तपाईं level (-नुहोस्, -नुहुन्छ) are
gender-neutral. Keep that level throughout.

## Buttons ("I/my")
"मेरो" + polite imperative: "Create my account" → "मेरो खाता बनाउनुहोस्", "Copy my words"
→ "मेरा शब्दहरू कपी गर्नुहोस्". First person past is gender-neutral in Nepali:
"I’ve saved them" → "मैले सेभ गरें" / "सुरक्षित गरें" **verify**. "Not now" → "अहिले होइन".
Long polite imperatives (-नुहोस्) make buttons long; a shorter "-्नुस्" form is
colloquial **verify** whether acceptable in UI.

## Plurals (CLDR)
`one` (exactly 1), `other`. With a numeral the noun often stays singular
("{count} नयाँ सन्देश"); -हरू is optional. Both forms may be identical.

## Names and brand
"Tinline", "Osvauld", "DID" in Latin. Case markers Nepali writes joined (-मा, -लाई)
go after a space with Latin names: "Tinline मा" **verify** house style.

## Native speaker must check
Hindi drift (है/हैं, करें, नहीं → Nepali छ, गर्नुहोस्, होइन/छैन), honorific level
consistency, loan spellings (Nepali writes English "v" as भ: भिडियो).
