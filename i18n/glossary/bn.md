# Bengali (bn), বাংলা

Read `india.md` first. Confidence: **medium**. Machine drafts are decent but drift
into sadhu-bhasha formality or Bangladesh vs West Bengal word choices inconsistently.

## Script and layout
- Bengali–Assamese script (Bengali letters), left to right. System fonts.
- Sentence end `।` (dari). Questions `?`.
- Audience: India (West Bengal, Tripura) and Bangladesh both read `bn`. Prefer words
  common to both; where they differ, note it. **verify** with a speaker from each side
  if possible.

## Register
**আপনি** (polite). Standard in apps and with anyone you're not close to. তুমি is
intimate. Use চলিত ভাষা (modern standard colloquial), never সাধু ভাষা.
Bengali verbs have no gender, so first person and third person are safe.

## Buttons ("I/my")
"আমার" + polite imperative or verbal noun: "Create my account" → "আমার অ্যাকাউন্ট
তৈরি করুন", "Copy my words" → "আমার শব্দগুলো কপি করুন". First person past works
(no gender): "I’ve saved them" → "আমি সেভ করেছি" / "সংরক্ষণ করেছি" **verify**.
"I agree" → "আমি রাজি" **verify** vs "সম্মত". "Not now" → "এখন নয়".

## Plurals (CLDR)
`one` (0 and 1), `other`. 0 is `one`: keep `{count}`. Bengali uses classifiers
(টি/টা) with numbers and no plural suffix: "{count}টি নতুন বার্তা" works for both forms;
both forms can be identical.

## Names and brand
"Tinline", "Osvauld", "DID" in Latin. Case endings: Bengali normally fuses -এর/-কে;
on Latin words, write them joined is unusual; prefer separate wording ("Tinline-এ"
with a hyphen is common in Bengali tech writing **verify**). For `{name}`, use
"{name}-এর" **verify**, or rephrase.

## Watch for
- সাধু forms (করিয়াছি), অনুগ্রহ করে on every line, ব্যর্থ / অবৈধ.
- Mixed তুমি/আপনি verb endings (-ো vs -ুন).
