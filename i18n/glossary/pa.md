# Punjabi (pa), ਪੰਜਾਬੀ

Read `india.md` first. Confidence: **medium**. Machine drafts are fair; common
problems are Hindi words in Gurmukhi letters and stiff formal phrasing.

## Script and layout
- **Gurmukhi** (Indian Punjabi). Left to right. System fonts. Pakistani Punjabi uses
  Shahmukhi (Perso-Arabic); `pa` here is Gurmukhi only.
- Sentence end `।` (danda). Tippi/bindi and addak (ੱ) spelling must be consistent.

## Register
**ਤੁਸੀਂ** (polite). Standard and warm in Punjabi; ਤੂੰ is intimate. Punjabi often adds
"ਜੀ" for politeness; do not overuse it in UI.

## Buttons ("I/my")
"ਮੇਰਾ/ਮੇਰੇ/ਮੇਰੀ" + polite imperative: "Create my account" → "ਮੇਰਾ ਖਾਤਾ ਬਣਾਓ", "Copy my
words" → "ਮੇਰੇ ਸ਼ਬਦ ਕਾਪੀ ਕਰੋ". First person is gendered in many tenses; use the
transitive past with ਮੈਂ (agrees with the object): "I’ve saved them" → "ਮੈਂ ਸੰਭਾਲ ਲਏ"
**verify**. "Not now" → "ਹੁਣ ਨਹੀਂ".

## Plurals (CLDR)
`one` (0 and 1), `other`. 0 is `one`: keep `{count}`.

## Names and brand
"Tinline", "Osvauld", "DID" in Latin; postpositions separate ("Tinline ਵਿੱਚ").
