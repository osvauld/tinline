# Santali (sat), ᱥᱟᱱᱛᱟᱲᱤ

Read `india.md` first. Confidence: **low**. Machine translation into Santali in Ol
Chiki is poor: expect transliterated Hindi/Bengali, wrong pronominal suffixes and
invented words. A Santali speaker who reads Ol Chiki must rewrite every string.

## Script and layout
- **Ol Chiki** (U+1C50–U+1C7F), left to right, as chosen in `languages.toml`. Ol Chiki
  is the script recognised for Santali in the Eighth Schedule; Santali is also written
  in Latin, Devanagari, Bengali and Odia scripts, and many readers know one of those
  better. **Product question**: Ol Chiki only?
- Font: **Noto Sans Ol Chiki**, bundled by the app. Check iOS and desktop use it.
- Ol Chiki has its own punctuation: ᱾ (mucaad, full stop) and ᱿ (double mucaad).
  Use ᱾ for sentence ends. Ol Chiki digits ᱐–᱙ may be shown by the platform; never type digits.

## Register
Santali distinguishes singular ᱟᱢ (am), dual ᱟᱵᱮ (abe) and plural ᱟᱯᱮ (ape) for "you".
I believe ᱟᱢ is the normal address to one person and that dual/plural forms carry
respect in some kin relations **verify**. Choose with a speaker; keep it consistent.
Santali verbs carry the subject (and object) as pronominal suffixes, not gender.

## Buttons ("I/my")
"ᱤᱧᱟᱜ" (my) + verb **verify**. First-person verb forms carry the suffix -ᱤᱧ, which is
gender-free.

## Plurals (CLDR)
`one` (1), `two` (2), `other`. Santali has a **dual**: write all three forms, e.g.
dual suffix -ᱠᱤᱱ, plural -ᱠᱚ on animates **verify** for inanimates like messages.
If an OS lacks the rule it uses `other`; make `other` acceptable for 1 and 2 if you can.

## Names and brand
