# Assamese (as), অসমীয়া

Read `india.md` first. Confidence: **medium-low**. Machine drafts often slide into
Bengali spelling and words. Assamese uses ৰ (ra) and ৱ (wa), where Bengali uses র and
ব/ও; a draft with র is almost certainly Bengali-influenced. A native speaker must check.

## Script and layout
- Bengali–Assamese script with Assamese letters ৰ (U+09F0) and ৱ (U+09F1). Left to right.
  System fonts include them; verify the desktop font has them.
- Sentence end `।`.

## Register
**আপুনি** (polite). Standard in public writing and apps; তুমি is familiar, তই intimate.
Assamese verbs mark person and politeness but not gender, so first and third person
forms are safe. Use the polite-2nd-person verb endings consistently (-ক / -ব).

## Buttons ("I/my")
"মোৰ" + polite imperative: "Create my account" → "মোৰ একাউণ্ট সৃষ্টি কৰক" **verify**,
"Copy my words" → "মোৰ শব্দবোৰ কপি কৰক". First person past is gender-free: "I’ve saved
them" → "মই ছেভ কৰিলোঁ" **verify**. "Not now" → "এতিয়া নহয়".

## Plurals (CLDR)
`one` (0 and 1), `other`. Assamese uses classifiers (টা, খন, জন) and plural suffixes
(বোৰ, সমূহ) — with a numeral, use the classifier, no plural suffix: "{count}টা নতুন বাৰ্তা"
**verify** classifier. Both forms may be identical.

## Names and brand
"Tinline", "Osvauld", "DID" in Latin. Case suffixes on Latin names: hyphen + suffix
("Tinline-ত") **verify**; or rephrase.
