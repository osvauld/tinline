# Odia (or), ଓଡ଼ିଆ

Read `india.md` first. Confidence: **medium-low**. Machine drafts in Odia are weaker
than Hindi/Bengali: expect odd word choices, Bengali/Hindi influence and stiff
officialese.

## Script and layout
- Odia script, left to right. System fonts on Android/iOS; check the desktop font
  (Odia conjuncts and the ୟ/ଯ distinction).
- Sentence end `।`.

## Register
**ଆପଣ** (polite). Standard in Odia apps; ତୁମେ is familiar, ତୁ intimate. Odia verbs
do not mark gender, so first and third person are safe.

## Buttons ("I/my")
"ମୋର" + polite imperative: "Create my account" → "ମୋର ଆକାଉଣ୍ଟ ତିଆରି କରନ୍ତୁ", "Copy my
words" → "ମୋର ଶବ୍ଦ କପି କରନ୍ତୁ". First person past: "I’ve saved them" → "ମୁଁ ସେଭ୍ କଲି"
**verify**. "Not now" → "ଏବେ ନୁହେଁ".

## Plurals (CLDR)
`one` (exactly 1), `other`. With a numeral Odia uses the classifier (ଟି) and no
plural suffix: "{count}ଟି ନୂଆ ବାର୍ତ୍ତା" **verify**. Both forms may be identical.

## Names and brand
"Tinline", "Osvauld", "DID" in Latin; case suffixes after a space or rephrase **verify**.
