# French (fr) review

Language: fr · File: `i18n/tm/fr.toml` (feeds `i18n/fr/{android,ios,desktop}.toml`) · Entries: 2053 · ok 1934 · fix 92 · native 14 · restructure 14 (1 of these is also fixed)
Reviewer: `i18n-review-fr` (2026-10-25), independent of the translator. Checked against `i18n/VALIDATION.md`, `i18n/glossary/README.md`, `i18n/glossary/fr.md` and `docs/design/VOICE.md`.
Confidence after review: **high** · Biggest issues: “carry a call” calqued as *porter* (19 strings), small-count agreement in non-plural strings, and English fragments that French can't take.

## Verdict

**Ship as a draft.** The translation is fluent, warm and consistent. It uses *vous* everywhere, uses the glossary terms (secret racine, mots de récupération, phrase secrète, associer/dissocier, hôte, raccrocher, chiffré de bout en bout) and its typography is clean: U+202F before `: ; ? !` and inside « », ’ apostrophes, `…`, and no “Veuillez”, *crypter*, “erreur” or exclamation marks. After the 92 fixes below, what remains is the restructure list. Those are English-side problems: no translation can make them correct. Fix the four that show broken French on screen first: desktop sign-up `{noun}`/`{value2}`/`{value3}`, the half-English terms line, the `{what}` field names, and `common_you` used as `{by}`.

Checks run after the edits: `python3 scripts/i18n_tm.py apply fr` (1520 / 1457 / 1135 entries) and `python3 scripts/i18n_gen.py --check` (passes, exit 0). A regex sweep finds no plain space before `? ! : ; »`, no straight apostrophe and no `...` in any French string.

## Translator's doubts, answered

| Doubt | Finding |
|---|---|
| Masculine default for *vous* agreement (retiré, connecté, bloqué, parti…) | Keep it. Apple, Google, WhatsApp and Signal all use the masculine default with *vous*; *retiré·e* would look out of place. Where a fix was cheap I rephrased to avoid agreement (“Pourquoi ce retrait ?”, “qui a fait chaque signalement”). A native speaker or product owner should confirm (native item 1). |
| “Stats pour les curieux” | Fine and on-voice for a desktop nerd panel. Optional alternative: “Statistiques détaillées” (native item 3). |
| Sound names | “Carillon classique” and “Lâche → tendu” work. “Triade frappée” reads oddly (native item 4). |
| “Impossible de …” repetition (52×) | This is the normal French UI pattern for “Couldn’t …” (Apple and Google use it). It is calm and not blaming. No change. |
| “Bon retour, {name}” | Changed to **“Bienvenue, {name}”**. On its own, *Bon retour* mostly means “safe trip home”. The idiomatic “Bon retour parmi nous” uses *nous*, which VOICE rules out, and “Content de vous revoir” needs a gendered adjective for the speaker (native item 2). |
